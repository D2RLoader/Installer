The code in this repository uses the GNU General Public License, version 3 (see [LICENSE](../LICENSE)).
The pictures in this folder do not.

- `portal.png` is the D2RLoader logo. All rights reserved by the D2RLoader project.
- The files in `icons/` and `generated/` are made from the logo. The same rule applies to them.
- The screenshots in `docs/images` show the installer window with the logo. The same rule applies to them.

The installer program contains these pictures. You may copy the installer unchanged, with its pictures.
If you change the installer or make your own version, take out the D2RLoader logo and use your own pictures.
Do not use the logo or the name "D2RLoader" to suggest that your version comes from the D2RLoader project.

To build the installer without the pictures, give `/DNO_ART` to the compiler, or delete `assets/generated`.
